Beaver Lamp

Care, safe use and maintenance instructions for your handmade wooden table lamp.

Important

For indoor use in dry locations only. Read these instructions before use and retain them for future reference.

Using your lamp

  • Place the lamp on a stable, flat, dry surface where it cannot easily be knocked over.
  • Keep it away from water, damp areas, direct heat sources and flammable materials. Do not cover the lamp or shade.
  • Keep the cable clear of sharp edges, hot surfaces and places where it may be trapped or pulled. Do not lift or carry the lamp by the cable.
  • Use the inline switch to turn the lamp on and off. Disconnect it from the mains before cleaning, inspection or changing the bulb.
  • Do not leave the lamp where children or pets could pull it over.

Bulb replacement

  • Use a compatible E14 LED bulb, rated for 230 V UK mains.
  • Maximum bulb rating: 5 W LED. Do not fit an incandescent or halogen bulb, or exceed the stated rating.
  • Switch off, unplug, and allow the bulb to cool before replacing it.
  • Do not force the bulb, twist the lampholder or alter any electrical components.

Bulb limit is the intended product specification and must be confirmed against final prototype testing before sale.

Cleaning and caring for the wood

  • Dust gently using a clean, soft, dry cloth.
  • Do not immerse in water or use sprays, solvents, abrasive cleaners or soaking-wet cloths.
  • Wood is a natural material: changes in tone and slight movement can occur with changing temperature, humidity and exposure to sunlight.
  • Avoid prolonged direct sunlight and damp or excessively hot positions.

When to stop using the lamp

Unplug immediately if the cable, plug, switch, lampholder or shade becomes damaged, loose, unusually hot, or if the lamp behaves unexpectedly. Do not use it again until appropriately inspected or repaired by a competent person. Do not attempt electrical repairs yourself.

Questions or issues?

Contact the maker through alexhillwoodworks.co.uk. When contacting us, include a description of the issue and any product identification shown on your lamp.
